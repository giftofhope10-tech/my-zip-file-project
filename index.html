About CalcVerse

Built for students, teachers and everyday problem-solvers

CalcVerse is a free collection of thirty online calculators covering mathematics, finance, geometry, statistics, physics and everyday tasks. Every calculator is built to do two things at once: solve your problem accurately and help you understand the maths behind it. That is why each page includes a working tool, the relevant formulas, at least two worked examples, a history section and a set of frequently asked questions.

Unlike ad-heavy calculator sites that copy the same template across hundreds of pages, CalcVerse gives every calculator its own genuinely different educational content. The Trigonometry page discusses the unit circle and the origins of trigonometry in astronomy and navigation; the Logarithm page covers John Napier and the pre-calculator era of computation; the Matrix page introduces linear algebra and the rules of matrix multiplication; and the Compound Interest page explains compounding and the mathematics of exponential growth.

All calculations run entirely in your browser using vanilla JavaScript — no data is sent to a server, nothing is stored, and the site works offline once loaded. The interface supports light and dark mode, keyboard input and mobile screens, and the whole site is lightweight enough to load almost instantly.
